What changes, and why

  1. 44×44 hit targets. Refresh and minimize become full-size buttons. The icon stays 20 px — only the tappable box grows, so nothing looks heavier.
  2. Minimize moves left. On a 390 px phone the top-right corner is the hardest place for a right thumb to reach. Dismiss is the most-used control in the bar, so it gets the easiest corner; refresh keeps the right.
  3. Two lines, not one. Title on the first line at 17 px, the attention count and working count beneath it. Today five items compete on one 56 px line and the status ends up at 12 px next to a red dot.
  4. A grabber. The expanded dock is a full-screen sheet, so it should dismiss the way every phone sheet does — swipe down, or tap the handle. The chevron stays for people who prefer a button.
  5. Bar grows 56 px → 60 px plus the grabber. That is the only vertical cost, and the tab row and cards are untouched.

Desktop is unchanged — every rule here sits inside the existing @media (max-width: 640px) block.
